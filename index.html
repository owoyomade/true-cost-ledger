Ledger

Every sale, purchase, expense and transfer in date order, with a running balance. Unpaid items are listed but don’t move your balance until they’re paid.

Sales

Log each sale or invoice. Link it to the purchase the goods came from and profit is worked out from the true landed cost.

Purchases & stock

Each time you buy stock or materials, log the full cost of getting it to your door: product, shipping, duties, broker fees. That gives you a true cost per unit.

Expenses

Running costs: rent, software, marketing, insurance, professional fees and so on. Add each payment when it happens, including every renewal.

Reports
